In-house technology

A crown in one visit, not three

The clinic is a CEREC practice — a crown is scanned, designed and milled on site while you are in the chair. No second trip, no temporary crown to babysit, no waiting a fortnight for a lab.

  • Digital scanning instead of gooey impression trays.
  • Same-day crowns and inlays milled and fitted in the same appointment.
  • Fewer visits for travellers — useful when you are flying home soon.
  • Precise colour matching for front teeth and bonding work.

Good to know

Questions patients ask before booking

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?+
The listing marks this clinic as “appointment required” and “appointments recommended”, so booking first is the safe option. Walk-ins are helped when the chair is free — call ahead on days you are short on time.
How long until my slot is confirmed?+
Booking here sends a request with a reference number. The clinic confirms by phone — usually the same working day — and will adjust the time if anything clashes. Until that call, treat the slot as provisional.
What does it cost?+
Fees are not published in the clinic's listing, so nothing is charged online and no price is shown here. You get a written quote at your examination before any treatment begins. Reviewers describe the prices as reasonable.
Can I get a crown done in one visit?+
The clinic advertises CEREC single-visit dentistry on its door, which means a crown can be scanned, milled and fitted the same day. Not every tooth is suitable — the dentist confirms after examining you.
I'm only in Cebu for a few days. Can you fit me in?+
Yes — reviewers repeatedly mention quick, efficient visits for travellers, including emergency root-canal work. Put your travel dates in the notes field and call the clinic so they can prioritise you.
Is the clinic accessible for wheelchair users?+
The listing confirms a wheelchair accessible entrance, parking lot and restroom, plus on-site parking and an on-site restroom.
How do I cancel or change my booking?+
Use “My bookings” next to the booking form: enter your reference or mobile number and cancel with one tap, then rebook a new time. You can also call (032) 253 3206. Please give as much notice as you can — the chair is in demand.
What should I bring?+
A valid ID, any previous X-rays or dental records, a list of medications and allergies, and your reference number. Arrive about ten minutes early for a new-patient check-up.
